At a glance

One scan, one picture, one clear next step

Most local business owners only check their ranking from their own shop or phone. A map scan checks the same search from many points across their service area and shows the result as a coloured grid, so they can see where they show up and where they don't.

ProfitMaps AI adds a list of competitors, an AI-written action plan in plain English, and a share link carrying your name and logo. You choose the business, send the link and set your own price.
