Product overview

What is ProfitMaps AI?

ProfitMaps AI is local SEO software that runs in your browser. It checks how a business ranks on Google Maps across its area, explains what to fix, and gives you the reports, websites and copy to sell that work as a service.

Intended purpose

Who ProfitMaps AI is made for

The seller aims ProfitMaps AI at freelancers, marketers, consultants and agency owners who work with local service businesses: plumbers, dentists, roofers, salons, restaurants, gyms, repair shops and similar trades.

It's also open to business owners who want to check their own Maps visibility. The seller says plainly that it isn't for anyone expecting guaranteed rankings or income.
