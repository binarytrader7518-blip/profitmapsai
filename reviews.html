Our notes

Strengths and trade-offs from the documented features

This is an editorial summary based on the official sales page, not a customer rating or a test of results.

Strengths

  • A focused workflow: scan, report, offer the fix
  • Grid scans show visibility across a whole area
  • Branded reports and share links on every plan
  • Websites and AI writing don’t use credits, up to plan limits
  • Starter credits never expire
  • No client log-in needed for scans or reports
  • A 30-day money-back guarantee with no forms, per the seller

Trade-offs to weigh

  • Launch pricing has ended. The seller says plans become monthly after launch, so confirm today’s terms.
  • Business Finder, white label and client accounts need a bundle plan
  • Basic limits drop after the first 500 buyers, per the seller’s FAQ
  • Scans use credits, and top-ups cost extra
  • AI copy needs fact-checking before publishing
  • No rankings or income guarantee
Before you buy

A six-point checklist

1

Know your buyer

Do you already have local businesses in mind, or do you need Business Finder (Gold or Platinum)?

2

Check today’s price

Look at whether checkout shows a one-time or monthly charge, and the exact plan you’re buying.

3

Estimate credit use

Scans and Business Finder searches use credits. Plan how often you’ll re-scan.

4

Plan your offer

Decide what you’ll sell after the report, whether that’s profile work, a website or content, and at what price.

5

Note the refund window

You have 30 days to ask for a refund. Try the main features early.

6

Keep claims honest

Show evidence and offer work. Don’t promise rankings, as the seller also advises.
