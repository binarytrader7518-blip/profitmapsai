ProfitMaps AI is a web app that scans Google Maps rankings for local businesses. It turns each scan into a report with competitors and an AI action plan, and includes tools for building websites and writing local marketing copy. Full overview.

Freelancers, marketers, consultants and agency owners who work with local businesses such as plumbers, dentists, roofers, salons, restaurants, gyms and repair shops. You can also use it on your own business. It isn't suited to anyone looking for guaranteed rankings or income.

You enter a business and a keyword. The app checks Google Maps results from a grid of points across the area (5×5 by default, up to 9×9 on higher plans) and shows the business's position at each point. The seller says a scan takes a few minutes. See the workflow.

It's software, so there are no ingredients. The "contents" are features: grid scans, competitor view, AI action plans, branded reports, an AI website builder, a Business Profile post writer, a local article writer, a keyword finder, ranking history, recurring scans and CSV export. The bundles add Business Finder, client workspaces and white label. Browse the focus areas.

The seller says no website or special skills are needed, and you don't need access to a client's Google account for scans or reports.

There are three plans. The sales page lists these one-time launch prices: Basic at $37, Gold Bundle at $297, and Platinum Bundle at $797, or $697 with the auto-applied $100 coupon. The seller says launch pricing ended on 27 October and that plans become a monthly charge afterwards (Platinum is listed at $297 a month). Check the checkout page for the current price. Compare plans.

Basic covers scans, reports and AI tools for up to 10 businesses. Gold adds Business Finder, the Pitch Pack, 9×9 scans, workspaces, team seats and 10 white-label client accounts. Platinum has the highest limits, with unlimited businesses, keywords, websites and Business Finder under fair use, and 100 white-label client accounts. See the comparison table.

Map scans and Business Finder searches use credits. Websites and AI writing don't, up to your plan's limits. Starter credits never expire, and you can buy top-ups in the app from $10.
